# Greenway trail reopens after two-year rebuild

The new Willamette Greenway boardwalk south of the boat ramp.

After 26 months of fencing, detours and more than a few muddy shoes, the Willamette Greenway trail between the Sellwood boat ramp and Oaks Bottom reopened on September 19. About 300 neighbors walked the new boardwalk on opening day, and the ribbon was cut by nine-year-old Amara Okafor, who wrote the most letters to the city asking when it would be finished.

The $4.2 million project replaced 1,400 feet of eroding gravel path with a raised boardwalk, two wildlife viewing platforms and lighting that switches off from 10 p.m. to protect nesting herons. Portland Parks says the boardwalk is designed to stay dry in a 25-year flood.

Thank you to the 167 people who volunteered at least one shift this year, and to the local businesses that sponsor the festival: Riverbend Market, Phở Riverbend, Sellwood Press and Oaks Bottom Bicycles. See you at the potluck! — Lauren Kim
