Under net metering, the energy your panels deliver to the grid is subtracted from the energy you take from it. Charges that depend on kWh use the net amount; the basic charge and assistance charge apply every month regardless of how much energy you use.

Unused credits roll forward month to month. Any credits remaining at the end of the March billing cycle are donated to the Cascadia lowincome assistance fund, as required by Oregon net metering rules.

Rooftop solar arrays like yours offset part of each month’s use.

The public purpose charge funds energy-efficiency programs and is set by the state at 3% of electricity service charges. The franchise fee is collected for the City of Corvallis for the use of public rights-of-way.

### Understanding your bill

- kWh (kilowatt-hour): the unit used to measure electricity; a 1,000-watt heater running for one hour uses 1 kWh.

- Net energy billed: energy delivered from the grid minus energy your solar system sent to the grid.

- Basic charge: a fixed monthly charge that covers metering, billing and customer service.

- Delivery charge: the cost of moving electricity over local poles, wires and substations to your home.
