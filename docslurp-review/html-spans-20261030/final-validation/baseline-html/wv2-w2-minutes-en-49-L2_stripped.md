¹ Transit Manager Kemi Oyelaran-Hart is not related to Councilmember Adebayo Oyelaran; disclosed for the record at the Councilmember’s request.## 5. Public hearing: Ordinance 26-14, sidewalk cafés on Aspen Avenue (second reading)

The Mayor opened the public hearing at 6:31 p.m. Community Development staH summarized the ordinance: permits for up to six tables per frontage, a minimum clear pedestrian path of six feet, seasonal operation from April 1 to October 31, and a fee of $18.00 per table per season. The Downtown Business Association supports the ordinance.

Public testimony: Aiko Hoshino (Hoshino Ramen) supported the ordinance and asked for winter operation with heaters; Grace WhitNeld (Accessible Larkspur) asked that the six-foot path be measured from the curb, not from the tree grates; Marco Bellandi supported the ordinance. The hearing was closed at 6:52 p.m. Council discussion focused on accessibility. Councilmember Oyelaran proposed an amendment requiring the clear path to be measured from the outer edge of tree grates and street furniture; the City Attorney conNrmed the amendment is not substantive and does not require a new Nrst reading. Councilmember Oyelaran moved to amend Section 3(b) as described; seconded by Councilmember Nguyễn. Amendment carried. Vote: Ayes 6, Nays 0, Abstain 0, Absent 1 (of 7 members).

Councilmember Kowalczyk moved to adopt Ordinance 26-14 as amended; seconded by Mayor Pro Tem Ibarra-Núñez. Motion carried. Vote: Ayes 5, Nays 1, Abstain 0, Absent 1 (of 7 members). Councilmember Strand-Eriksen voted no, stating that winter operation should be considered before adoption.

Finance Director Castellanos presented Budget Amendment No. 1 (Exhibit B). The amendment appropriates $660,500 from unassigned reserves for storm-drain repairs, extended park maintenance, the Route 4 shuttle pilot and the Aspen Avenue streetscape. Reserves remain at 21.4% of General Fund expenditures, above the 17% policy minimum.

Exhibit B (as presented): FY2027 Budget Amendment No. 1, screenshot from the Finance Department slide deck

Councilmember Kowalczyk asked whether the Route 4 pilot would continue after June 2027. The City Manager answered that the pilot is limited to nine months and will be evaluated against ridership targets of 120 boardings per weekday. Councilmember Nguyễn requested that the evaluation include surveys in Spanish and Vietnamese.

Mayor Pro Tem Ibarra-Núñez moved to adopt Resolution 26-59 approving Budget Amendment No. 1; seconded by Councilmember Oyelaran. Motion carried. Vote: Ayes 5, Nays 0, Abstain 1, Absent 1 (of 7 members).
