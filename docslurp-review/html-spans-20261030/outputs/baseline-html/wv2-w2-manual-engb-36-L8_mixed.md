## CALDER HYDRO

# CH-6 Smart Irrigation Controller

Installation and User Guide for models CH-6, CH-6 Pro and CH-6-O (outdoor). Firmware 2.8 and later.

Automatic watering keeps borders healthy while using up to 40% less water than a hose.

## Before you start

Thank you for choosing Calder. Please read this guide fully before installing the controller and keep it somewhere safe. The installation of the controller itself is straightforward, but the wiring of valves in the garden may require some experience with outdoor electrical work. If in doubt, ask a competent installer; a list of Calder-approved installers is available at calderhydro.example/installers.
