## Welcome to Tacoma

Five years ago this summit started as a video call between three city data teams who wanted to stop reinventing the same budget dashboards. This November we expect more than 900 people in the room and another 300 online, from 41 cities, 9 tribal nations and 14 countries.

This year’s theme, “Data that survives contact with the public,” is about the unglamorous work that keeps open data useful: documentation, privacy reviews, stable APIs and plain-language reports. We hope you leave with at least one idea you can ship before the end of the fiscal year.

— Dana Whitfield, Program Chair, and the Cascadia Civic Data Collaborative board
