# Information

Form BL-210 · Use this form to renew an existing business license for the period July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027 and to report changes to ownership, location, mailing address or business activity. A separate form (BL-100) is required for a new business or for a change of legal entity.

## Instructions

1 2 3 4 5

Type or print clearly in blue or black ink. Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. Renewal applications must be received by April 30, 2026. Applications received after that date are subject to a late fee of 25% of the renewal fee per month, up to 100%.

If your business moved, complete Section 3 and attach a zoning clearance (Form Z-4) for the new location.

Sales tax accounts are administered separately; changes reported here do not update your sales tax license.

Fees are non-refundable once the application has been processed.

### File online and save time

Most renewals can be filed online at larkspurfalls.example/licensing without paper forms. Use this paper form only if you are also reporting a change of location or ownership.
