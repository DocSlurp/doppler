# Autumn Menu

Barley fields on the Compton estate, where our malt is grown.

Welcome to the Wheatsheaf. We are a seventeenth-century coaching inn on the edge of the Cotswolds, and we cook with what our neighbours grow: beef and lamb from Fosseway Farm, vegetables from the walled garden at Weston Park and ales brewed four miles down the road in Hook Norton.
