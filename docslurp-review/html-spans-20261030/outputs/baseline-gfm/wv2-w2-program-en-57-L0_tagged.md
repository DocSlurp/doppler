## Welcome to Tacoma

Five years ago this summit started as a video call between three city data teams who wanted to stop reinventing the same budget dashboards. This November we expect more than 900 people in the room and another 300 online, from 41 cities, 9 tribal nations and 14 countries.

This year’s theme, “Data that survives contact with the public,” is about the unglamorous work that keeps open data useful: documentation, privacy reviews, stable APIs and plain-language reports. We hope you leave with at least one idea you can ship before the end of the fiscal year.

### — Dana Whitfield, Program Chair, and the Cascadia Civic Data Collaborative board

### Nguyễn Thị Lan

Ho Chi Minh City, where the Saigon Open Data Lab maps budgets by ward.

Lan founded the Saigon Open Data Lab in 2019 after eight years as a statistician at the city’s finance department. Her team publishes ward-level budgets in Vietnamese and English.

### Ingrid Solberg

Ingrid leads the data platform team at Bergen Kommune, which has run a public API for building permits since 2017. She maintains several open-source tools for cleaning Norwegian address data.

### Rafael Montoya Ruiz

Rafael drafts most of his opinions on public-records law at home in Bogotá.

Rafael is a professor of information law at Universidad de los Andes in Bogotá and a former adviser to Colombia’s transparency secretariat.

### Eleni Papadaki

Eleni coordinates the 311 service-request feed of the City of Athens and wrote its public data dictionary.
